# Facts that go out of date

Every fact on a concept, entity or insight page is timeless, dated, or a
pointer to where the truth lives. A count or a status in the present tense
with none of these ("the suite has 240 tests", "it is currently the largest")
reads as true for ever. Write the date, `(as of 2026-10-07)`; cite the dated
episode in the same paragraph; or say where the number lives and leave it
out. Episodes and decisions are dated pages and need nothing more.
`brain check` lists such lines under `undated`; it warns and never fails.
