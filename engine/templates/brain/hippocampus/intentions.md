# Intentions

What the owner asked to be reminded of, one line each under the heading
below, in the form `- <what to do> when <when>`. The `when` is a day
(`2026-11-01`), a minute of one (`2026-11-01 10:00`), a repeat (`every day`,
`every monday`, `every month`, each with a time or without: `every monday
09:00`) or an event in a few words. A dated one shows in the session briefing
once its time comes; a repeat comes due again at the next round after it was
last done; an event is held against every new input by `brain fit`. End a
line with `(done)` or `(dropped)` when it is over, with the day and what
happened: `(done 2026-11-03: the setup was confirmed)`. A repeat is ended
only by `(dropped)`. Written by `/remind`.
