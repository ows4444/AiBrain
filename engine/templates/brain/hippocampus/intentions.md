# Intentions

What the owner asked to be reminded of, one line each under the heading
below, in the form `- <what to do> when <YYYY-MM-DD or an event>`. A dated one
shows in the session briefing once its date comes; an event one is checked
against every new input by `/ingest`. End a line with `(done)` or `(dropped)`
when it is over. Written by `/remind`.
