# Policy

What this brain may do to itself with nobody there. One line each under the
heading below, written by you and by no one else: `- action (why, and since
when)`. `brain act` lists the actions. One that only reads may always run and
needs no line. One that changes the brain runs alone only when it is named
here, and git can undo what it did. One that reaches outside the brain, or
cannot be undone, can never be allowed here: `brain check` fails on such a
line. To take a leave back, remove the line.
