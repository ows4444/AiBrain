# Fixture brain

A small, fixed brain on a public topic (how people learn and forget), used by
`brain eval` to measure retrieval and answers after every engine change. Its
pages summarise well-known studies in a few lines each; they are test data,
not anyone's notes. Change it only together with `../questions.json` and
`../baseline.json`.
