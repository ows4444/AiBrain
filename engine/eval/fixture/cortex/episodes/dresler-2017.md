# Mnemonic training reshapes brain networks to support superior memory (2017)

## What it is

A training experiment: adults with ordinary memory practised the method of
loci for thirty minutes a day over six weeks.

## Claims

- This source says the group trained in the method of loci more than
  doubled the words recalled from a list of 72, and an untrained group and
  a group given other training did not
  (supports:: [[method-of-loci]]).
- It says the gain from the method of loci was still present four months
  after training ended.
- It says patterns of brain connectivity in the trained group moved
  towards those of memory competitors.
- It notes the method of loci was tested on word lists only.

## Candidates

- Transfer of mnemonic training - whether the gain reaches material other than lists
