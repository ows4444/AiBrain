# Why I stopped handing out one-topic worksheets

## What it is

A blog post by a secondary school mathematics teacher about a term of
worksheets built on interleaving.

## Claims

- This source says the teacher replaced one-topic worksheets with
  interleaving: every sheet carried problems from earlier topics among the
  new ones (supports:: [[interleaving]]).
- It says lessons with interleaving ran slower and pupils complained that
  interleaving made them feel they had learned nothing.
- It reports better end-of-term results than the year before, with one
  class and no comparison group.
- It says interleaving costs the teacher time: sheets have to be rebuilt
  each week.

## Candidates

- Cumulative worksheets - every sheet returns to earlier topics
