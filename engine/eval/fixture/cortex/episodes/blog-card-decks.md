# How I memorise a shuffled deck in two minutes

## What it is

A blog post by a hobbyist who memorises shuffled decks of cards for sport.

## Claims

- This source says the whole routine is the method of loci: each card has
  a fixed image, and images are placed two to a stop along a route through
  the house the author grew up in (supports:: [[method-of-loci]]).
- It says the method of loci fails first at the stops, not the images: a
  route with vague stops loses cards.
- It says a route can be reused after a day, once the old images fade.
- It claims anyone can reach five minutes a deck with the method of loci
  in a month. No evidence is given beyond the author.

## Candidates

- Person-action-object - a system for turning three cards into one image
