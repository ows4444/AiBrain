# Routes to remembering: the brains behind superior memory (2003)

## What it is

A brain-imaging comparison of ten people placed highly in memory
championships with ten matched controls.

## Claims

- This source says the competitors' brains did not differ in structure
  from the controls', and their general ability was not exceptional.
- It says nine of the ten used the method of loci, and that the method of
  loci explains the difference in what they could do
  (supports:: [[method-of-loci]]).
- It says regions used for spatial memory and navigation were more active
  in the competitors while memorising, as the method of loci would
  predict.
- It concludes that superior memory here is the method of loci applied
  with long practice, not a gift.

## Candidates

- Trained not gifted - exceptional memory as a learned strategy
