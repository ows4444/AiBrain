# Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks (2006)

## What it is

A meta-analysis of 317 experiments comparing spaced study with massed study.

## Claims

- This source says spaced study beat massed study across the experiments it
  pooled (supports:: [[spacing-effect]]).
- It says the best gap between study sessions grows with how long the
  material must be retained: roughly ten to twenty percent of the retention
  interval, so a test a year away wants sessions about a month apart.
- It reads the gains as slowing the [[forgetting-curve]]
  (extends:: [[forgetting-curve]]).

## Candidates

- Optimal gap - the best spacing depends on the retention interval
