# Test-enhanced learning (2006)

## What it is

An experiment with students who read a prose passage and then either reread
it or took practice tests on it.

## Claims

- This source says students who took practice tests remembered more of the
  passage after one week than students who reread it, although rereading did
  better on a test five minutes later: the [[testing-effect]].
- It says students who reread predicted they would remember more, so how
  fluent material feels is a poor guide to how well it is learned.

## Candidates

- Illusion of fluency - rereading feels like learning more than it is
