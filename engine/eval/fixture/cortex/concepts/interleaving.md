# Interleaving

## In one paragraph

Working through different kinds of problem in a shuffled order, instead of
finishing one kind before starting the next. It feels slower and scores
lower on the day, and more is kept afterwards.

[[rohrer-taylor-2007]] in mathematics; [[kornell-bjork-2008]] with paintings;
