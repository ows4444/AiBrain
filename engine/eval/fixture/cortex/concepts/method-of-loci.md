# Method of loci

## In one paragraph

A way of remembering an ordered list: each item is turned into an image and
placed at a stop on a familiar route, then read back by going along the
route in the mind.

## Where it came from

[[maguire-2003]] found it in use among competitors; [[dresler-2017]] trained
beginners in it; [[blog-card-decks]] describes it step by step.
