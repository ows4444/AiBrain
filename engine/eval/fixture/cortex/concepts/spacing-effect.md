# Spacing effect

## In one paragraph

The same amount of study is remembered longer when it is spread over several
sessions than when it is massed into one. The best gap between sessions
depends on how long the material must be kept.

## Where it came from

[[ebbinghaus-1885]] found it in his own lists; [[cepeda-2006]] pooled 317
experiments; [[wozniak-sm2]] builds a review schedule on it.

## What supports it

All three sources above.

## What argues against it

Nothing consolidated yet.

## Open questions

Whether the gains hold for skills as well as facts.
