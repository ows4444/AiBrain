# Desirable difficulties

## In one paragraph

Some conditions lower performance while learning and raise what is kept
later. Learners usually prefer the easier condition and rate it as the
better one.

## Where it came from

[[kornell-bjork-2008]] names the idea and measures the misjudgement;
[[rohrer-taylor-2007]] shows the same pattern.
