# Testing effect

## In one paragraph

Pulling an answer out of memory strengthens it more than reading it again.
Practice tests feel harder than rereading and look worse at first, but what
was tested is remembered better a week later.

## Where it came from

One salient experiment, [[roediger-karpicke-2006]].
