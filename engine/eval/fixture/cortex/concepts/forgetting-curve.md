# Forgetting curve

## In one paragraph

Memory for newly learned material drops fast at first and then more slowly:
most of what is lost goes within the first hour or day, and what survives
that fades only gradually. Each review flattens the curve again.

## Where it came from

[[ebbinghaus-1885]] measured it on himself; [[cepeda-2006]] reads spaced
study as a way of slowing it.

## What supports it

Both sources above.

## What argues against it

Nothing here.

## Open questions

How much of the curve holds for meaningful material rather than nonsense syllables.
