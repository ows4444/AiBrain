# Spaced retrieval

## The question

Do testing and spacing add up, so that tests spread over time beat both?

## What the sources say

The [[spacing-effect]] pages show spread sessions beat massed ones; the
[[testing-effect]] page shows tests beat rereading. [[supermemo]] already
schedules tests, not rereads, at growing intervals.

## Where they disagree

They do not; no source here tested both together.

## Current position

Probably they combine, which is what flashcard schedulers assume.

## What would change it

A study that varies spacing and testing in one design.
